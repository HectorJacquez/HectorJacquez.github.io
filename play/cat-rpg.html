Nico's Bizarre Adventure

An early demo of a Mario & Luigi-style RPG starring my two cats, Shadow and Nico. A hub, a few rooms, a boss. Built in Godot, running right here as a web export. Click the game once so it gets your keyboard.

Controls

Arrow keysmove
Spacejump
DShadow's action
SNico's action
Eswap leader
Qability (Nico's Cat-Ball)
X / Esccancel / menu

A controller works too — the buttons map like a Switch pad.

What to do

Jump-bump the fish in the hub to save. Rooms branch off the hub; clear the enemies in them and the boss arena opens. In battle it plays like Mario & Luigi: jump is the heavy option, swipe answers specific attacks, and timing your presses matters on both offense and defense.

It's a demo — things end after the boss, and some edges are rough on purpose.
