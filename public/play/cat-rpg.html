Nico's Bizarre Adventure

An early demo of a Mario & Luigi style RPG starring my two cats, Shadow and Nico, with a hub, a few rooms, and a boss. It's built in Godot and running right here as a web export, so click the game once so it picks up your keyboard.

Controls

Arrow keysmove
Spacejump
DShadow's action
SNico's action
Eswap leader
Qability (Nico's Cat-Ball)
X / Esccancel / menu

A controller works too, and the buttons map like a Switch pad.

What to do

Jump-bump the fish in the hub to save. The rooms branch off the hub, and once you clear the enemies in them the boss arena opens. In battle it plays like Mario & Luigi, where jump is your heavy option, swipe answers specific attacks, and your timing matters on both offense and defense.

It's a demo, so things end after the boss and some of the edges are still rough.
