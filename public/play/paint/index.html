Paint on a Model

Draw straight onto a 3D model with mouse or touch. Drag on the model to paint, pick a sticker and tap to stamp it, or drag the background (or pick orbit) to spin it; scroll or pinch to zoom. Nothing is uploaded or saved anywhere; refresh to reset. It runs on the same painting core as my hub's Paint page.
